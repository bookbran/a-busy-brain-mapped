  1. Everything that isn't work, kept close. It's the reason the rest of this map exists.

  2. A Portland Career helps people find work that fits and thrive wherever they are in their career journey. Our coaches walk alongside them through self-insight, discovery, guided mapping, well-researched plans and the job search itself.

  3. Ursina and I co-wrote Art & Mind, a book about making decisions, out since May 2026. Anyone reading our book can model their important life choices in Decision Art, our app, and come to clarity. Our learning platform is also in use at two universities.

  4. Astrolabe is a place to dream, connect and build. Danny and I make it so people can reach their goals with support, accountability and tools of their own. It keeps teaching me to empower myself and those around me, now that our dreams are more within reach than ever.

    • A globe made of notes In a member's own brain every light on the globe is a note, placed by what it is about. Regions grow where the work piles up, and the busiest places glow brighter the more they are used.
    • Region art A region can get its own sculpture, drawn from lines and tuned until it reads at globe size. - planned from the region itself - scored and checked - judged by a separate reviewer before it goes up
    • The chat at the center You talk, and the brain keeps the useful parts. The globe lights up as notes are read and written, so you can **watch the work happen** instead of wondering what it did.
    • Rituals you can press A way of working you repeat becomes a button: a **morning catch-up**, a *wind-down*, a Monday list. Each one waits for you, and nothing runs on its own.
    • The seatbelt With the seatbelt on, it stops before anything sends, buys, deletes or publishes, and asks first. Taking it off is a choice the member makes in plain words.
    • Courses through a region Big builds are charted as a voyage of stops on the globe. A small sailboat shows which stop the work is on right now, and each stop links to its build tracker.
    • Two chats, one build An orchestrator plans the next slice while a developer builds it, side by side. In *Speedboat* mode they hand the work back and forth on their own until the course is done or a real decision needs the member.
    • A control tower When several builds share one computer, heavy jobs wait their turn in a single lane, so the machine never chokes and nothing collides.
    • Handoffs that remember Every session ends with a closeout and a prompt for the next one, so a fresh chat picks up exactly where the last one stopped.
    • More than one engine It runs on Claude Code today, and the same brain is learning to work with other engines too, so the brain belongs to its member and not to any one tool.
    • A landing page that draws itself The public page was rebuilt pass after pass until a visitor could watch the product draw itself, one region at a time.
    • This world, shared The globe you are turning was published from a private brain. Only the words approved for it ever left the computer, and the lights are placed by a seed, not by the notes beneath.
    • Built a slice at a time Most of it was built in long evening sessions, one slice at a time, each one shipped and looked at before the next began.
    • Why I build it My own mind runs in many directions at once. Astrolabe is the place I wished I had: one world where ideas, people and work stay connected, and nothing has to be organized first.
    • What it keeps teaching me A good system meets you where you are and keeps up. Building this keeps reminding me how much more possible our dreams become with the right support around them.
    • How I like to work Fast slices, a working thing on the screen by the end of the first sitting, then the next step designed from what I see. Astrolabe is built that way, and it helps its members **build that way too**.
